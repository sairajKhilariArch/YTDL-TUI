01 / Features

Powerful where it matters.

A practical workflow for choosing media and managing downloads, wrapped in an interactive terminal interface.

⌘

Interactive terminal UI

Navigate a purpose-built interface powered by Ratatui and Crossterm.

▤

Playlist selection

Select individual videos in a batch or download an entire playlist.

⇩

Video or audio

Download video as MP4 or audio-only as MP3 with configurable bitrate.

◷

Live progress

Track download progress with a progress bar and live console log stream.

▧

Organized files

Customize folder structures by playlist, uploader, upload year, or a custom name.

CC

Subtitles & chapters

Use subtitle and chapter embedding options when preparing downloaded media.
